          About Urbids

          About How it works Safety FAQ Privacy Contact us

          Urbids is exploring a resale marketplace for quality pre-owned products. Buyers can discover prices through auctions or choose a clear Buy Now price.

          Detailed listings, seller and buyer reputation, and flexible handover options help people make informed choices. Inspection is suggested when the product or its risks call for it, not as a requirement for every item or transaction.

          Urbids does not own inventory, professionally refurbish items, verify or authenticate products, or guarantee an outcome.

          This is a research prototype. Listings, people, ratings and transaction outcomes are fictional; nothing here is a real offer to buy or sell.

          Urbids is a working name. Domain and trademark availability have not been checked.

          For buyers

          1. Compare item details, condition and seller reputation.
          2. Bid to discover a price or choose Buy Now.
          3. Choose from the handover options available for that item.
          4. Inspect when its category and risks make a check useful.
          5. Confirm receipt to complete the transaction.

          For sellers

          1. Describe the item clearly, including its condition.
          2. Receive bids or Buy Now interest.
          3. Agree a suitable handover with the buyer.
          4. Build reputation through completed transactions and ratings.

          All steps on this site are simulations for research, not real transactions.

          • Condition is seller-declared. Urbids does not verify ownership, authenticity or product condition.
          • Inspect higher-risk items when appropriate, especially smartphones and laptops. Check the model, disclosed defects and included items.
          • Prefer busy public meetup locations. Do not share a home address in a listing or feedback.
          • Keep transaction actions on-platform so the agreed terms, handover and any issues remain together. This is not a guarantee of protection.
          • Never share an OTP, PIN, password or payment credentials.
          • Report a material mismatch, such as a wrong model or undisclosed fault, through the transaction's issue option before confirming completion.
          • Prototype deposits, payments and refunds are simulated. Do not transfer money or enter real payment details.
          How does bidding work?

          Place the displayed next bid. The highest eligible accepted bid at auction close wins. A valid bid in the final two minutes extends the auction by two minutes; further valid bids in each renewed final two-minute window extend it again.

          Who chooses the bid increment?

          The seller chooses the auction's increment. Each next bid adds that fixed amount; buyers do not choose a custom manual amount.

          How does automatic bidding work?

          Set a private maximum and Urbids bids only when needed, using the seller's increment and never exceeding your limit. You can increase it later. Equal eligible maxima favour the earlier maximum. Other buyers and the seller do not see your limit. It is saved in this browser, not in a secure online account.

          What happens if I win?

          Confirm within six hours, review any simulated reservation deposit and choose an available handover option. This prototype does not arrange a real sale.

          What is the reservation deposit?

          A proposed commitment step, credited toward the final price on completion. The prototype illustrates no deposit below ₹5,000, ₹200 from ₹5,000 to ₹15,000, and ₹500 above ₹15,000. No money is collected; these values require review before a live service.

          Is a meetup mandatory?

          No. Available handover options depend on the listing and item. Eligible items may use simple collection; public meet-and-inspect is available when appropriate. Shipping is only a research simulation.

          When should I inspect?

          It depends on the item and its risks. Phones and laptops may benefit from technical checks; furniture from a condition and dimensions check; appliances from an operating check; and fashion from a condition check. Books and collectibles may need only a simple condition check. Follow the listing's guidance; inspection is not authentication or a guarantee.

          What if the item differs from its listing?

          Use the transaction's issue option to report a material mismatch before confirming completion. A wrong model or undisclosed fault differs from a change of mind. Issue handling and any refund shown here are simulations, not a promise of recovery.

          What are second-chance offers?

          After an eligible failed sale, a seller may offer the item to a previous bidder at that bidder's last visible accepted bid. Offers are made one at a time and expire after 12 hours. They are simulated here.

          Is this a real marketplace yet?

          No. Each visitor uses an independent browser-local simulation. Bids, listings and activity are not shared with other visitors. Clearing browser data removes prototype activity. No real payments occur and no real accounts are created; browser-local activity should not contain sensitive information.

          Prototype records are saved in this browser. There are no real accounts, and other browsers or devices do not share your activity.

          Listings, profiles, bids, watchlists and feedback are stored locally in this browser. Other testers and browsers are not connected, so bids and activity are not shared.

          Do not enter sensitive information in feedback, listings or other fields. Use no real names, contact details, addresses, full device identifiers or payment information. No real payment data should be entered anywhere.

          Feedback is not sent to a research team. During a facilitated session, let the researcher know if you saved feedback; this form does not send a message. Clearing this site's browser data removes local prototype records.

          The public website host and external image providers receive requests when pages or images load. Browser-local prototype records are not uploaded by the app; this notice does not describe those providers' own practices.
